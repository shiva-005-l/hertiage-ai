# User Manual: HeritageAI

Welcome to HeritageAI! This guide will help you navigate and use the platform's features effectively.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Access**: Open the application in your web browser.
2. **Language Selection**: Choose your preferred language from the selector in the header to enjoy a localized experience.

## 📸 Monument Recognition

To identify a heritage site:
1. Navigate to the **"Scanner"** or **"Recognition"** tab.
2. Click the **"Upload Image"** button or use your device's camera.
3. Wait for the AI to process the image.
4. View the identified monument's details, including history and significance.

## 🤖 AI Heritage Chatbot

To interact with the smart assistant:
1. Click on the **Chat icon** in the bottom right corner.
2. Type your question about a specific monument or historical period.
3. Use voice input if available for a hands-free experience.

## 🚩 Reporting Issues

Help us preserve heritage:
1. Go to the **"Report"** section.
2. Provide a description of the issue (e.g., vandalism, maintenance needed).
3. Upload a supporting photo.
4. Submit the report to notify the authorities.

## 🎙️ Audio Guides

Listen to immersive stories:
1. Select a monument from the list.
2. Click the **"Play Audio Guide"** button.
3. Ensure your volume is up and enjoy the multilingual narration.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

- **Login Issues**: Ensure your internet connection is stable.
- **Camera Access**: Grant the application permission to use your camera for monument recognition.
- **Reporting Errors**: If a report fails, try again with a smaller image size.
